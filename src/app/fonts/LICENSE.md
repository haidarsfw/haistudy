# Fonts

Self-hosted copies of the latin subset served by Google Fonts, used by
`src/app/layout.tsx` through `next/font/local`.

| File | Family | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `plus-jakarta-sans-latin-wght.woff2` | Plus Jakarta Sans (variable, 500–700) | fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans |
| `inter-latin-wght.woff2` | Inter (variable, 400–700) | fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter |
| `poppins-latin-{400,600,700}.woff2` | Poppins | fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins |
| `geist-mono-latin-wght.woff2` | Geist Mono (variable, 400–600) | fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist+Mono |

All four families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), which permits bundling and redistribution
with software.
